Meet Scott

Harness racing runs deep in the Betts family. Scott's grandfather trained and drove Standardbreds, and his father was a veterinarian who worked on them, so he grew up around the sport.

Scott studied biology and entrepreneurship in college, and it was his grandfather who suggested buying a $5,000 fillies and mares claimer. That first horse turned into a career. He has been training since 2014, working for others in the summers before going out on his own, and built the stable from a single horse into a barn at The Meadows that regularly sits near the top of the trainer standings.

Scott is hands-on with every horse in the barn, and he is a skilled blacksmith who does his own shoeing. It is a family operation: his brother Tim works in the barn and handles the business side, and his mother helps with barn work and errands.

The barn's best-known name is Phoenix Warrior N, the 2018 Meadows Horse of the Year. Today the stable keeps a tight group of pacers racing at The Meadows and Northfield Park, with new horses joining all the time.
