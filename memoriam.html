In Memoriam

Phoenix Warrior N · Our Honor Defend

Phoenix Warrior N

2018 Meadows Horse of the Year

Phoenix Warrior N was the 2018 Meadows Horse of the Year, and the horse that put the Betts barn on the map.

He gave Scott, his family and everyone who cheered him home some of the best moments this sport can offer. Fast, tough and full of heart, he was a champion in every sense of the word.

Thank you, Phoenix. You will never be forgotten.

Our Honor Defend

Lost In Time filly · trained by Scott Betts

Our Honor Defend was a Lost In Time filly trained by Scott and co-owned by Scott, Keith Gisser and Kerry McCullough.

On October 4, 2025 she won the sophomore filly pace at the Buckeye Stallion Series Finals at MGM Northfield Park, coming from sixth at the three-quarter pole to win by a nose at 24-1 in 1:54.2, with Case Bateson in the sulky.

  • Wins5 in 33 starts (before that final)
  • In the money16 of 33 starts
  • Earnings$86,728 (before that final)

Record as reported before the 2025 Buckeye Stallion Series Finals. Source: Harnesslink and US Trotting News.
