How it works

Four Steps to the Winner's Circle

Scott trains every horse himself, and the family runs the barn. Owners get straight answers and real updates.

1

Pick a horse

Browse the barn and pick the horse you want in on, or tell us your budget and we will suggest one.

2

Choose your share

Own as little as 1% or as much as is available. Your share sets your part of the costs and the purse money.

3

Get licensed

Owners need to be licensed to race. We walk you through the paperwork so it is quick and painless.

4

Enjoy race day

Follow every start, get updates from the barn, and join us in the winner's circle when your horse wins.

The numbers

What It Costs

Training is $75 a day per horse. Owners split the horse's costs and its purse earnings in proportion to their share, so a 10% owner pays 10% of the bills and collects 10% of what the horse earns.
